Service-on-demand

Balaji Electro Controls also provides Service-on-demand option for addressing emergency maintenance or for trouble shooting during downtimes.

Service-on-demand is a reactive maintenance and not a recommended option, unless the nature of the installation or business is not critical.

Balaji Electro Control provides professional support services 24x7 ensure the problem is addressed in a timely manner. The service is provided on a per-call basis to address specific, timely needs.

Service-on-demand is more expensive, as it requires immediate response and is a one-off case to attend. Service charges are subject to the nature of the services, components to be replaced, and other consultancy services, if necessary.
